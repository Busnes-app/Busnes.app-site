KyPost / Email

Your mailbox.
Your devices.
Your server.

Keep the mailbox you already use, with a private hub that connects your devices and puts you in control of your email.

At a glance

Is it for you?

For people and small teams who want to run their own email interface while keeping their existing IMAP and SMTP provider.

How you use it
A Linux host with Docker Compose, an IMAP/SMTP account, and a properly secured server address. The browser client ships with the server.
Availability
The maintained KyPost site lists the server, Android and Linux as open beta, with iPhone and Mac clients in testing. Check that site for current installation options and client availability.

Inside KyPost

Built with a purpose.

01

Keep your provider

KyPost sits between your existing mailbox and your devices. You do not have to become a mail-delivery operator to use it.

02

Pair your devices

Connect a client with a QR scan. Each device gets its own credential, so a lost phone can be revoked separately.

03

Choose how mail is handled

Configure sorting on your server and choose notification options that fit how much information you want on a lock screen.

How it fits together

Know where the pieces live.

Before you get started

Your server stores mailbox credentials and mail data. Its operator is responsible for access, updates and backups. Notification providers may process delivery data when enabled.

Read the KyPost privacy policy. For data on an independently operated installation, contact its operator.

1. Who we are

Busnes.app is a d/b/a operated by Matthew Beacher. In this policy, “Busnes.app,” “we,” and “us” refer to Matthew Beacher doing business as Busnes.app. Contact us at privacy@busnes.app.

2. Scope and roles

This policy covers the Busnes.app public website, project inquiries sent to us, maintainer-operated services, and official Ky applications or services where Busnes.app determines why and how information is processed.

It does not make Busnes.app responsible for an independently operated customer application, KyPost Server, or other self-hosted software. The person or organization running that installation normally decides what information it collects, why it is used, who can access it, and how long it is retained. That operator should provide its own privacy notice.

3. This website

The Busnes.app website is a static public site. It does not use advertising, analytics, cookies, account forms, or third-party embedded content. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Project inquiries and correspondence

When you email Busnes.app or contact us through GitHub, we may process your name, email address, organization, message, attachments, technical context, and related correspondence. We use this information to answer you, determine whether we are a fit, scope or deliver work, provide support, maintain our software, and investigate security reports.

We do not need customer lists, database exports, financial records, credentials, private keys, or other sensitive files to assess an initial inquiry. Please do not send them through ordinary email. If a project requires sensitive material, we will agree on a safer exchange method later.

Email providers and GitHub may process this information under their own terms. We do not put inquiry text into advertising profiles or third-party analytics.

5. Ky applications and services

Ky is a family of software projects built by Busnes.app. The exact information handled depends on the application and its operator. A self-hosted installation may process account information, sessions, credentials, messages, notes, secrets, contacts, files, device registrations, preferences, diagnostics, backups, and logs. The operator chooses its infrastructure, integrations, users, backups, retention, and deletion practices.

For KyPost specifically, a server may process mailbox credentials, messages, headers, attachments, contacts, public keys, encrypted or wrapped private-key material, device registrations, push tokens, filter rules, classification results, diagnostics, and logs. Official clients may keep local copies of mail, contacts, attachments, keys, pairing information, and notification registrations.

KyPost’s built-in classifier runs using the model configured on the operator’s server. The default design does not send inbox contents to a cloud AI provider. An operator can change the software or connect other services, so users should ask that operator about its actual configuration. Busnes.app does not receive KyPost mail or account data merely because someone installs or uses KyPost.

If an operator enables a Busnes.app-operated push relay, the relay may process a server identifier, device token, delivery provider, notification payload, timestamp, and limited security or failure-log information to deliver Apple, Google, or UnifiedPush notifications. Generic notifications do not contain sender names, subjects, or message bodies. Preview content is included only when the user enables previews.

6. Where information may go

  • Website infrastructure: the hosting provider and its infrastructure providers.
  • Support and correspondence: GitHub and relevant email providers when you contact us.
  • Push delivery: Cloudflare, Apple, Google, or a UnifiedPush provider when the corresponding KyPost option is enabled.
  • Project operations: service providers needed to host, secure, maintain, back up, or support an agreed application.
  • Legal and safety: authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service security.

Some recipients may process information outside your country. The safeguards available for a particular transfer depend on the service, the operator’s location, and the provider’s terms.

7. Retention and security

We keep information under our control only as long as we have a legitimate business, support, security, legal, or dispute-resolution reason to retain it. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable. Project records may be retained for the duration of an engagement and for a reasonable period afterward to support maintenance, accounting, legal obligations, and continuity.

Maintainer-operated push relay request content is held only in memory for delivery where the service design permits; security and failure logs are retained for 180 days. Self-hosted operators determine their own retention and backup schedules.

We use access controls, encryption in transit, restricted service credentials, backups, and limited logging appropriate to the information we control. No system is completely secure. Never send passwords, recovery shares, private keys, or credentials by ordinary email.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a privacy regulator.

Send requests concerning Busnes.app, this website, or maintainer-operated services to privacy@busnes.app. For information held by a self-hosted Ky application, contact that application’s operator. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

9. Children

Busnes.app services and this website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. If you believe a child has provided information to a maintainer-operated service, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post revisions here and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects a maintainer-operated service, we will also provide notice through an appropriate project or service channel before the change takes effect when reasonably possible.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@busnes.app

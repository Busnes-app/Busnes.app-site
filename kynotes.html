At a glance

Is it for you?

For people and teams who want to keep notes and attached material on their own infrastructure and are comfortable operating a web application.

How you use it
A server environment configured according to the deployment guide, a browser, and a backup plan covering both the database and encrypted attachment blobs.
Availability
The server source and deployment documentation are available. Review the repository for current changes and setup requirements; this page does not promise a managed hosting plan or app-store release.

Inside KyNotes

Built with a purpose.

01

Encrypted content

The server stores encrypted notes and attachments. Your browser handles content decryption.

02

Use your own infrastructure

Deploy the service yourself and choose the access, storage and operational setup that fits your environment.

03

Plan for recovery

Administration includes recovery-key pinning, scheduled backups, local sealed copies and restore drills. Attachment storage has its own mirror and recovery path.

How it fits together

Know where the pieces live.

Before you get started

A database capsule alone does not recover all attachment data. Follow the restore guide and configure the separate encrypted blob mirror.

Read the Busnes.app privacy policy. For data on an independently operated installation, contact its operator.

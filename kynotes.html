KyNotes / Notes & attachments

A place for
private thoughts.

Encrypted notebooks for text, ink, attachments and tasks, with team sharing and content decrypted in your browser.

Open source (MIT) · Tested
Read setup guide ↗View source ↗▶ Watch: KyNotes in 2:31 (YouTube)

At a glance

Is it for you?

For people and teams who want to keep notes and attached material on their own infrastructure and are comfortable operating a web application.

How you use it
Docker Compose, a current browser and separate administrator and everyday accounts. KyIdentity is optional. Back up both the database and the encrypted blob store that holds attachments and note history.
Availability
Source builds, a published container image and deployment documentation are available. Review the repository for current changes and setup requirements; this page does not promise a managed hosting plan or app-store release.

Inside KyNotes

Built with a purpose.

01

Encrypted content

Notes and attachments are encrypted in your browser. Shared team notebooks use their own keys, wrapped for each member, so neither the server nor the administrator who created your account can read them.

02

Notebooks your way

Organize notebooks into sections, pages, subpages and section groups. Canvas pages add text boxes and pen ink; task and work-queue views keep actions alongside your notes.

03

Built for teams

Use local accounts, or connect KyIdentity for sign-in and directory access changes. Administrator accounts manage the service separately from everyday accounts and cannot open note content.

04

Link another device

Add a browser or device with a one-time key bundle, released only after you confirm a matching check code on both screens.

05

Plan for recovery

Administration includes recovery-key pinning, scheduled backups to KyRecovery, local sealed copies and synthetic restore drills. Complete recovery also needs the separately configured ciphertext blob mirror.

06

Use your own infrastructure

Deploy the service yourself and choose the access, storage and operational setup that fits your environment.

How it fits together

Know where the pieces live.

Before you get started

A database capsule alone does not recover attachments or note history. Back up the encrypted blob store separately, with restic or the built-in mirror, and follow the restore guide.

Read the Busnes.app privacy policy. For data on an independently operated installation, contact its operator.

About Busnes.app

Software is personal.
So is the work.

Busnes.app is operated by Matthew Beacher. It brings a simple idea to custom software: build around the way people work, and take responsibility for the result.

Matthew Beacher

The person

I build the Ky projects and the software behind Busnes.app. The work here spans email, encrypted notes, password storage and the shared services those applications depend on.

Busnes.app is deliberately small. When you bring a workflow problem here, the conversation is close to the person doing the work.

What guides the work

Useful beats oversized.

Understand first

The unusual part of your process may be important. Learn why it exists before trying to automate it away.

Keep the scope honest

A useful first release should have clear boundaries. Being explicit about what is left out makes the result easier to build, use and maintain.

Make the work inspectable

The Ky repositories provide a look at the software and its documentation. Product pages distinguish current capabilities from work in progress.

The last mile

You probably don’t need another platform.

Maybe the important part of your business is still in a spreadsheet. Maybe your current system does 90% of what you need and makes the remaining 10% miserable.

Maybe information gets copied from one system to another because they do not talk. Maybe customers or employees need one simple workflow that nobody sells.

That last mile is what we build.

Built on Ky

The custom part should be the part that’s actually custom.

Ky is the reusable foundation behind Busnes.app applications: common pieces for identity, security, recovery, deployment and the other things that should not be reinvented for every business.

That lets each application spend more of its complexity on the part that makes that business different.
